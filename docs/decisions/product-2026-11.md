# What Quietkeep is, the product stated whole, November 2026

## What Quietkeep is

Quietkeep keeps what a person puts down and brings it back by rule. It was built from research on neurodivergent need and is aimed at a condition, which the research catalog names as "low capacity, high demand, interruption, and engagement that varies", so it serves anybody while that condition holds. It does two things for that person. While the strain lasts, it lets them cut what they see down to what they can act on, as far as one thing, by their own hand. When they lift their head, it shows what moved while they were down, already set in its place among everything they hold, with all of it in view and findable. The collision catalog's frame is "This app is a PROSTHESIS, not a treatment", and it says that "what survives is external scaffolding at the point of performance". The premise is the research catalog's: "The target is not rescue. It is cost."

## The findings

Each is in its source's own words, with the grade the source gives it and what it requires of the screen. C is the collision catalog by entry, R the research catalog by section, H the survey of horizon models, V the note on planning vocabularies and N the notes. The research catalog's rule for grades is the one used: a design resting on emerging or community evidence is built so it can be removed, and a negative finding is a prohibition.

- C1, strong. "the knowledge is intact, the launch mechanism is not"; "Activation cost is highest when the task is large, vague, or aversive"; made worse when "A long list raises the activation threshold of every item on it, and priority matrices demand a comparison at exactly the moment comparison is impossible." Requires: where work is started, one small concrete first step and no long list to compare.

- R5, established. "Habits are cued by stable context far more than by intention or motivation." Requires: each part stands in one place, always.

- C19, community. "the quiet presence of another person dramatically lowers initiation cost — not through supervision but through co-regulation". Requires, as a refusal: the product never imitates a person and never keeps attendance.

- C21, community, resting on C3's finding. "Prospective memory (Einstein & McDaniel) fails hardest for intentions with no natural cue"; "The choice degrades to obsessive checking or total evaporation". Requires: what is waited on from another person has a cue of its own and comes back by rule.
- C22, community. "the person is too depleted to work and too guilty to recover, achieving neither"; "rest is not a luxury input here, it is maintenance". Requires, as a refusal: nothing on the screen compares one day with another, and nothing counts what was done over time.
- C23, community for the whole; strong for the parts under it. "working memory is famously small (Miller, 1956; refined to roughly four chunks by Cowan, 2001)"; "a block of N questions costs more, cognitively, than the same N questions asked one at a time on different days"; "psychological reactance (Brehm, 1966) is among the most-replicated findings in social psychology"; "directive framing produces more resistance than choice-preserving framing"; and the comparison of asking in the flow against asking at the door "has not been tested, for this population or any other." Requires: the screen works with nothing placed; nothing is asked in a block or at the door; a question comes one at a time, where its answer would change what is shown, and can be declined; and nothing the person must hold in mind at once runs past about four.
- C24, strong for the mechanisms, ungraded for the product claim: "the product claim built on top of them has no grade in this set, because nothing has measured it". "they ask it bounded by a moment — where they are, how long they have, who is in front of them, how much is left in the day"; unaided, the person must "hold the room, the clock and the list in their own head and cross-reference". On reading a screen: "A person reading a titled item on a screen is doing recognition, not free recall from nothing". On a stated length: "it is a reason not to trust a self-reported number as a filter boundary". On many options: the effect is "concentrated where options are hard to compare and the chooser has no clear prior preference". On who is present: "a specific person standing in front of someone is the most distinctive, focal event-based cue of the three". Untested: "whether specifying a situation (one tap, one field) carries anything like the activation cost of scanning a list, long or short". Requires: the person can say where they are, how long they have and who is with them, and what is shown is bounded by it; a thing with no label fits everywhere; a stated length never removes a thing as though an estimate were fact; things shown together are easy to tell apart; and the whole of it stays small, declinable and reversible, since its cost against scanning is unmeasured.

- C25, strong for the mechanism and its cost. "A promise made TO someone else is the same intention, aimed the other way, and it is not spared the mechanism"; it fails with "no artifact, no scheduled moment, nothing left on the desk once the conversation where it was promised has ended"; and it "is discovered by the person it was owed to, and read by them as a fact about the reader". Ungraded: "whether SEEING a standing list of what you owe other people helps you keep more of it, or teaches you to stop opening the list". And an audience works against it: "identity-related intentions NOTICED by another person were acted on LESS than ones nobody knew about". Requires: what was promised to another person has a cue of its own and comes back by rule; it is shown plainly, with no age, tally or rising tone, to the person alone; and showing it as a standing list is a reversible bet.
- C26, community for the practice, strong for one mechanism under it that was measured on other people. "A list made in advance, at a calmer moment, and consulted rather than composed removes that one step"; reused without change, "the same handful of items quietly stop paying out". Requires: wants are written ahead and consulted under strain, never composed then; never earned, never tracked for use.
- C27, strong. "When the price rises, the recording does not get slower. It stops."; "At all memory loads, reminder-setting was reduced when it was more effortful."; and "the accuracy benefit it would have delivered is attenuated when it is done." The toll is as harmful when it is "rebuilt the whole gate one screen later, at sorting". Requires: putting down is one act that asks nothing; placing a thing is nearly as cheap and never required.
- C28, moderate, one well-powered experiment. "If that promise is kept perfectly, the person stops holding them."; after fully reliable support people "reduced PM-related thoughts, redistributed attention to the ongoing task, and showed reduced PM retrieval when reminders were unexpectedly withdrawn."; and "The 50% condition did not substitute at all — uncertainty kept people maintaining internally". From it: "Uncertainty about whether the store is holding something produces the same behavior as a store that is not holding it", and "Reliability the reader cannot CHECK is 50% reliability from the inside". Requires: the person can check at any time that everything is held and where; every part that holds something says that it does; and the person holds a way out of the store that they have used. The same entry declines to carry the finding that a plan quiets an unfinished goal, since "the only sources reachable from here for that literature's current standing are secondary aggregation"; so R1's line on relief from a plan is not used below.
- C29, strong. "the extent of underconfidence, or metacognitive bias, also increased as memory load increased"; "the fuller the pile, the more a person underestimates themselves"; "nothing on the outside can tell which error a given reader is making". Requires, as a refusal: nothing comments on how much a person puts down or on whether a thing needed putting down; and the proof matters most when the most is held.

- C30, strong for the cost of monitoring. Monitoring is "a process that is attentionally demanding and therefore induces a cost to the ongoing task"; much of what is carried has no cue because "the thing being waited for is a change in the world rather than an event in front of them"; and "monitoring costs, continuously, and people ration it by context". Requires: a state of affairs that is being watched can be put down as what it is, with where it stands and what would change it in the person's own words, so the watching is held outside the head; it is never turned into a repeating demand, and the product never says a condition has been met.
- C31, strong for the ordering, moderate for the proportions. "forgetting turns out to be a minority cause, and the biggest single reason is that the person reconsidered and did something else"; the reasons given were "change in priorities or rescheduled 33.6%, lack of time 17.2%, fatigue or lack of motivation 15.2%" with "forgetting 9.6%"; "the things people set reminders on still failed, for reasons a reminder cannot address"; and more reminding leaves the two largest causes alone: "no time, and no fuel — are untouched by being told again". The authors call re-deciding "a rational and adaptive response to changing circumstances". Requires: a date that has gone by is shown as a decision to confirm or change, never as a lapse; nothing measures how much was done and nothing asks why; stated importance orders nothing; and because time and fuel are the larger causes, the person can see how much is set against the days ahead and can cut what is shown when fuel is low.
- C32, strong for the failure and for the prohibition, contested for whether any record helps. "the accounts diverged at encoding, and no future date was ever attached to the divergence"; "71% of witnesses who discussed it went on to mistakenly recall items acquired only from the discussion"; "failures are directory failures rather than memory failures"; and couples were "worse than impromptu pairs when a memory structure was imposed on them". Requires: no second person's account is ever stored or shown beside the person's own; at most a pointer that a named person holds the rest of something, carrying no content, kept as a reversible bet.
- C33, strong, measured on a spoken handover between two people with a read-back. "the medical-error rate fell 23%"; the transfer carries "how sick, the summary, what to do, what might happen and what to do then, and a synthesis read back by the receiver"; "the thing that most reliably does not survive is not the open work at all: it is what was decided and closed"; and "the evidence does not carry across to anything the app could do alone". Requires: what was decided is kept and can be seen; and an account of how things stand, given to another person or to the person coming back, runs in that order, state, what is live, what might happen, what was decided, and is not a list of open items. The use for a person coming back to their own store is borrowed and unmeasured.
- C34, moderate for the construct, and "the link this entry would need is unevidenced rather than refuted", so the entry rules it "Background, not grounds". What it describes of tools stands on R10b: "every planner they try assumes one professional context", and "a thing belonging to two domains has to be duplicated or filed under whichever was thought of first". Requires, on R10b's ground: one surface holds every domain a person works in, with no wall and no mode between them, and a thing may belong to more than one. - R2, established, with two negative findings. "Delay is not a time-management failure; it is short-term regulation of negative affect around the task."; "anything that increases aversion increases delay"; "Extrinsic rewards and scores can undermine intrinsic motivation for a task the person already valued."; and of two popular ideas, willpower as a tank "failed to replicate" and "Working-memory training does not transfer". ## The day it must carry, from what done means

- D1. A walk-in. One act takes down what was said with who said it, and what stands with that person is in sight at once. (N done; S4, S9)
- D2. A staff call. Naming the meeting or the people in it shows what stands with each, what changed since it last met and what was decided, and that account can be carried out of the product in H7's order. (N done; H5, H7, S9)
- D3. Every suspense. A date another person is counting on lives here with what it feeds, its latest start and the room left. (N done; S8)
- D4. The desk paper. Anything can be put down in one act, and everything held can be seen and found, since paper comes back the moment either fails. (N done; K1, K3, H8)

## The screen

One surface holds everything, in one fixed order of parts. Above the middle the order follows what the notes ask for, what matters on top, with S1, S4 and H2; below it the order is judgement.

1. The line. One field, first, always on the screen, and beside it a control that finds any held thing by its words. (K1, S4, D1, D4)
2. The proof. One line beside the line: everything put down is held. It opens to where everything is and when each thing comes back, in groups whose counts sum to the whole, and it is never narrowed. (K3)
3. What moved. Present only when something moved since the person last looked: dates gone by as decisions, what came round, what another person did or still holds, a watched state marked as changed. At most three stand here, with the true number said and one act that sets the rest aside without loss, and each is also marked, in words, where it stands in the parts below. (H1, H2, H3, H5)
4. Now. The way back first, if one stands. Then one or two unalike things, each with its first act, where it sits and why it is here. Then, in words, the next fixed thing today. Passing over a thing records nothing. (S1, S3, S4, H4)
5. Days. Today, the six days after, then the days out to thirty that hold something. Each day shows what is set against it, so that a full day is seen to be full. A thing with a start and an end is a stretch. A date others lead up to shows its latest start and the room left. Today's row holds what is ready again. (S8, D3, C31)

## The widths

One control, always in the same place, sets how far down that order the screen goes, and only the person moves it. (S2, S5)

- Rest shows parts 1, 2 and 9, and asks nothing.
- One thing shows parts 1 and 2 and a single thing from part 4, in larger type, with its first act, the next fixed thing today in words, and three ways on: done, not this, enough for now.
- Today shows parts 1 to 4 and today's row of part 5. The product opens here, so nobody is asked how they are.
- Everything shows all nine.

Coming back after an absence opens at Today with part 3 at its head, and one turn shows everything, with what moved marked in place.

## Narrowing

A second control beside the first takes a place, a length of time, the people present, a meeting or a role, singly or together, and narrows every part at every width. A line says what is on and how much is set aside, and one press shows the other side. Naming a meeting or its people turns part 6 into the account D2 asks for. (S9, D1, D2)

## The sheet

Any title opens that thing's one sheet: its note, when and during what it was put down, its first act, its dates, its people, its roles, its place, what it sits under and leads up to, where it stands and what would change it, and what was decided. Closing returns to the same place at the same width. (K4, K5, S10, H7)

## The acts

Putting down, on the line. Starting, from Now, which turns the width to One thing for that thing until it is put down. Placing, on the sheet or in a sorting sitting, never required. Settling what moved: confirm, change or let go.

## How it keeps the laws

Each of the ten is kept as the notes word it. On the fourth: nobody has to open the widest view to plan a day, work is started from Now at every width, and the widest view is the same surface carried further and holds nothing to tend. On the eighth: what stands first after an absence is bounded as that law bounds it.

## Refused

A late mark, a score, a streak, a percentage, or any count of what was done or of the person (R2, C22, C31, N laws). An order of importance (C5, and C31 found that importance did not predict what got done). Anything read off what a person passed over (C2, C29). A time of day on a date, and fitting work into gaps (C9, C24). A setup flow, a template to choose, or a meter of how much is placed (C23, the literature with outcome numbers). A question about mood or state (N refusals, R6). A nudge to put down less, or a verdict that a thing need not have been put down (C29). Accountability imitated or sent over a channel, and anything shown to another person by the product (C19, C25). A second account of what another person said (C32). A reward to be earned, or any record that a want was used (C26, C20, R2). A mode or profile built on a trait (C34). A width the screen changes for itself, or a switch it starts (S2, S5). Urgency made up or raised (C13, N). A claim to train the person or to bring relief (R2, N refusals). A part for tending the store, with no dragging into order, no priority field and no tidying pass (C18, community, which calls its own refusal cheap and reversible). And hue carrying pressure, which is the product's standing rule and rests on no finding entered here.

## What no finding settles

Each of these is judgement and is built so that it can be reversed: that the width is one control with four positions, and their names; that starting a thing turns the width to One thing; the numbers, two at Now, seven days and thirty; the order of parts 5 to 9; roles as the first grouping of part 8; marking what moved in place as well as at the head; and giving the meeting's account as a narrowing of part 6.

## What stays unmeasured

The narrowing (C24), the standing list of promises (C25), the pointer to who holds the rest (C32) and the order of an account (C33) are each ungraded or borrowed, and each is built to be taken out whole. By the literature with outcome numbers, nothing here is known to work until it has carried a day.

## What done means

The notes' own measure, quoted under N, done, and nothing else: the product is finished when it has carried thirty working days in a row.
