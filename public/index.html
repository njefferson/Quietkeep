                                                        How it works

                                                        The one thing it offers you

                                                        Two things at a time, never a list, and never the same two by accident — they are picked to be unalike so you can prefer one rather than compare them. Not this costs nothing and records nothing.

                                                        Finishing, and stopping

                                                        Finish something and the screen settles: it names what you did and waits. Nothing arrives until you ask for it. That is enough for now ends the session the same way finishing does — stopping is not failing.

                                                        How heavy, and how the day is going

                                                        Mark anything light, ordinary or heavy, and tell Quietkeep the day is low, steady or sharp. It changes which things come forward, never how many. Nothing is counted and nothing is worked out about you from it.

                                                        When the screen is too much

                                                        Just one thing shows a single item in larger type with nothing else — no reasons, no counts, nothing moving. You turn it on and off; it is never turned on for you.

                                                        When and where you meant to do it

                                                        Any item can carry a line in your own words — after I put the kettle on — shown back exactly as you wrote it. Never required, never checked.

                                                        How long things take

                                                        Note a rough guess if you want one. Where something has been timed, Quietkeep shows the two ends of what actually happened, never an average, and never a comparison with your guess.

                                                        Add it to your home screen

                                                        Quietkeep runs in the browser, but it is better on your home screen: it opens like an app, works with no signal, and it is where the browser can hold onto your planner for good.

                                                        • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
                                                        • Android or a computer (Chrome / Edge): open the browser menu and choose Install, or use the install icon in the address bar.

                                                        Installing asks to allow notifications. Allow it — on Apple devices that is what lets the browser keep your planner instead of clearing it, and it is the only reason it is asked. Quietkeep never sends notifications. Nothing is uploaded: it is the same app with its own icon.

                                                        Help

                                                        Short answers to the things people ask. Tap one to open it.

                                                        Every path through Quietkeep — every way in, through and out, drawn as the steps you take.

                                                        How do I get something out of my head?

                                                        Type it in the box at the top and press Hold it. One line is enough — you are only getting it down.

                                                        What happens to everything I put in?

                                                        Quietkeep offers each one back when you have a moment, one at a time, with a small choice. Nothing is lost while it waits.

                                                        How does it decide what to show me next?

                                                        It hands you one next thing with a short list behind it, never the whole pile. Not this costs nothing.

                                                        What if a date has already gone by?

                                                        Nothing is ever marked late or overdue. A passed date becomes a card asking when you would like to look at it again.

                                                        Will it remind me?

                                                        Not by itself — it sends nothing. Send the item to your calendar — Send to my calendar, under Things you can do — and the calendar reaches you.

                                                        Is my writing private? Where does it live?

                                                        On this device, and nowhere else. No account, no sign-in, no server, and nothing collected about you.

                                                        Everything stays on this device. No account, no sign-in, and no server holding your writing, because there is no server. Nothing about you is collected.

                                                        Everything stays on this device — no account, no sign-in. When your devices trade changes, the copy that travels is sealed here first, with a key only your devices hold; the relay it passes through cannot read a word of it. Nothing about you is collected.

                                                        How do I back it up, or move to a new device?

                                                        Export a copy under Your data saves everything as one file; Bringing a copy back reads it on the new device.

                                                        Can I keep two devices in step?

                                                        A separate edition, Quietkeep Sync, does that — sealed on your device before anything leaves, with a key only your devices hold.

                                                        There is a separate edition, Quietkeep Sync, that does exactly that — sealed on your device before anything leaves, with a key only your devices hold. The default app you are in never contacts anything at all.

                                                        Yes — this is Quietkeep Sync, the edition that does exactly that: sealed on this device before anything leaves, with a key only your devices hold. Pairing lives under “Keeping two devices in step” in Your data.

                                                        Why does it work this way?

                                                        Every choice here comes from research on memory, attention and motivation. The full reasoning, with sources, is in Planning for Humans.

                                                        Moving to a new device, or restoring after something went wrong. Choose an exported file and Quietkeep will tell you what is in it before anything changes.

                                                        The record itself

                                                        Everything Quietkeep shows you is worked out fresh from one append-only record of what happened. You can read that record — every line, in plain words, newest day first. Looking changes nothing.

                                                          Things you let go

                                                          Letting go is a decision, and decisions can be revisited. Everything you let go is here, newest first — open one and “Keep it after all” brings it back with a clock of its own.

                                                            The Not Now ledger

                                                            Things other people asked of you, that you decided not to carry. Each is parked — it comes back on its own day, quietly — and each is kept, so when the same request comes back you can point at the decision instead of making it again.

                                                              The journal

                                                              Somewhere to write that is not a list and asks nothing of you. Entries are scrambled with a passphrase only you know, so they cannot be read from this device’s storage, from a backup, or by anyone Quietkeep syncs through. Nothing here is ever counted, searched, or brought back at you.

                                                              It stays scrambled until you open the journal again.

                                                                Bringing work in from another planner

                                                                Takes a TaskPaper or CSV export — OmniFocus, or anything TaskPaper-shaped. Projects keep their contents.

                                                                  Clearing things out

                                                                  Two different things, and not the same favour. Save a copy first — it is the only way back.

                                                                  Things you can do

                                                                                                  What Quietkeep is
                                                                                                  
  
                                                                                                  
    A planner that holds what you put down and brings it back on its own, so you
    do not have to remember to look. It lives entirely on this device: no account,
    no sign-in, no server.
  
                                                                                                  
  
                                                                                                  
    A planner that holds what you put down and brings it back on its own, so you
    do not have to remember to look. It lives on your devices, and what they trade
    is sealed with a key only they hold.
  
                                                                                                  

  
  
                                                                                                  What it is not
                                                                                                  
  
                                                                                                  
    It is not a medical or therapeutic tool, and nothing in it
    is advice. It never scores you. It cannot reach you on its own, so reminders
    go through your calendar. It holds only what you put in.
