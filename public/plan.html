Right now

Phase 2 — the horizon comes downcomplete

2.18.0 and 2.18.1. A page for what you are working toward — your goals, areas and outcomes, each with what it is carrying and how often it comes back. Behind a control, never the screen you land on.

The empty ones are on it, and that is the point. A goal with nothing under it yet is still a goal. The app could always work out which ones had nothing feeding them, but only ever raised it as something needing attention — which is a different thing from being able to look at what you have and decide what goes under it. Finish everything under a goal and it stays on the list saying it holds nothing, which is exactly when it would otherwise disappear.

1The app can be enteredcore fixed

Not done here: asking where you are at the moment it would change the answer. That was the original design and it sits downstream of this — it can wait for a real device rather than be guessed at.

2The horizon comes downcomplete

Done: a goal or an area can be made, and it can carry a rhythm that brings the work under it back. A design document that proposed giving a role its own clock is corrected — the app refuses that on purpose, because a thing that can be late is a demand and a role is an identity. The clock rides the goal instead.

Filing many things under one goal at once turned out to already work — three of this phase's four steps did. What was missing there was only that the list of places never said which kind of place each one was.

No promote button, no screen to climb to, and nothing counted or scored.
